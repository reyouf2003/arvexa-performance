Policies & Client Protection

Privacy Policy

ARVEXA Performance respects the confidentiality of information submitted through this website or shared for the purpose of discussing or delivering services.

Information we may receive

  • Company and contact details submitted through the enquiry form.
  • Business, financial, operational or performance information voluntarily shared for a consultation or engagement.
  • Communication records reasonably required to manage the relationship.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, assess requirements, prepare proposals, perform agreed services and communicate with the client. We do not sell client information.

Confidentiality

Information provided by a client will be treated as confidential and will only be accessed or used to the extent reasonably required to evaluate or perform the agreed work, subject to the applicable agreement and law.

Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration or disclosure. No online system can be guaranteed to be completely risk-free.

Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the business relationship, legal, accounting or dispute-resolution requirements, after which it may be securely deleted or anonymized where appropriate.
